FAQ

Subscription questions, answered.

Can I cancel anytime?

Yes. There's no lock-in contract — message us on WhatsApp and we'll cancel or pause your plan before your next billing date.

Can I change my plan later?

Of course — upgrade to Plus or Family (or downgrade) whenever your needs change. Just let us know on WhatsApp.

Do the discounts apply to prescription medication?

Discounts apply to over-the-counter products, supplements, and skincare. Prescription pricing follows standard pharmacy regulations, but delivery is still free on every order.

How do the free consultations work?

Plus and Family members get consultation credit each month — just book on our Book Consultation page and mention your subscription.

Not sure which plan fits?

Tell us what you usually order and how often — we'll recommend the right plan.
