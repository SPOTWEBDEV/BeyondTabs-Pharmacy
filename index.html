What is CellGevity, and why do people ask about it?

As with any supplement, CellGevity isn't a substitute for medical treatment, and what works for one body may not suit another. Watch the short video for an overview, then let's talk before you buy a single capsule.

FAQ

Questions people ask us often.

Do I need a prescription to order?

Some medications require a valid prescription, which you can send us via WhatsApp or bring in-store. Over-the-counter items and wellness products don't need one — our pharmacists are still happy to advise.

Do you deliver outside Lagos?

We currently deliver within Lagos, with select arrangements available outside the state. Message us on WhatsApp with your location and we'll confirm.

How do I place an order?

Send your prescription, product name, or a photo of what you need to our WhatsApp number, and our team will confirm price, availability, and delivery or pickup.

Are your products genuine?

Yes. Every product is sourced from verified manufacturers and licensed distributors — we do not stock counterfeit or expired medication.

Can I speak to a pharmacist before buying?

Always. Call, WhatsApp, or visit us in-store — our pharmacists are available to answer questions before you commit to any purchase.
