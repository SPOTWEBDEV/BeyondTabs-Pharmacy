About BeyondTabs

Beyond the counter. Beyond tabs.

A pharmacy built on one idea: healthcare should feel personal, patient, and completely honest — every single time.

Our Story

From a single counter to a trusted name.

BeyondTabs Pharmacy started with a straightforward mission: make quality healthcare accessible without the confusion, upsell, or wait. Today, from our location at Prince Ebeano Supermarket, Platinum Way, Jakande, we serve individuals and families across Lagos with genuine medication, wellness products, and pharmacist-led advice — in-store, on WhatsApp, and at your doorstep.

We named ourselves BeyondTabs because we believe a pharmacy's job doesn't end at handing over a pack of tablets. It's in the explanation, the follow-up question, the honest "you probably don't need this" — the parts that go beyond the transaction.

A Word From Our CEO

Meet Pharm. Mrs. Adaora Nnadi

Pharm. Mrs. Adaora Nnadi founded BeyondTabs Pharmacy on a simple belief: healthcare should feel personal, not transactional. With years of clinical pharmacy experience, she leads a team committed to genuine products, honest advice, and care that goes beyond the counter, beyond tabs, beyond the transaction.

"Every customer who walks through our doors is someone's mother, father, or child. We treat every prescription like it's for our own family."
